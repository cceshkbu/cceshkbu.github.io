Dr. Ou Alison is a Research Assistant Professor at Hong Kong Baptist University, holding a PhD in Sports and Health Sciences and has an academic background in social policy, criminology, and conflict resolution.
Alison's research focuses on healthy ageing, health equity, and the built environment, with particular interests in understanding how socio-cultural and environmental factors influence the well-being and physical activity of older adults.
With over 7 years of experience in academic research, Dr. Ou has contributed to international and interdisciplinary research projects across Hong Kong, Canada, and New Zealand, whose work connects research and policy to develop inclusive, evidence-based solutions that support ageing societies and promote intergenerational cohesion.
